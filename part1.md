StudentShare is a peer-to-peer software company specializing in academic file sharing. Having a P2P software model means users of StudentShare will ultimately build our platform through participation and interaction. Thus, to succeed as a startup, we will need to establish a strong user base within an academic setting. More specifically, over the course of the next five years, our primary goal is to establish a minimum of 100 students at all CSU and UC campuses within California. If we can achieve this goal, we will be one step closer to accomplishing our long-term goal to create a student hub that supports academic success through file and resource sharing.

The idea of StudentShare originated from our own student experiences. When we first considered creating our platform, we were driven by the need for improved file sharing. File sharing is often needed within a group setting, and many services available require a login that slows down the process of document transfers. We wanted to create a technology that would allow instant access to document-sharing features, skipping the login process and allowing for a more efficient collaborative experience. We wanted quicker, less personal file sharing. As we delved deeper into the student experience and what we thought was missing from it, we identified a few other areas of interest that led us to conceive of StudentShare as not only a file-sharing service, but also a student hub that could be used to meet other academic needs. StudentShare was created to address many of the digital challenges that occur frequently within an academic setting. 

As previously mentioned, our mission as a software company is to support academic success through file transfer and resource sharing. As a P2P student focused network, we are dedicated to steering the development of our platform towards responsible academic file sharing, resource acquisition, and academic support through design features that facilitate copyright protection, uphold academic integrity, and reduce misinformation. Our core values align with this sentiment, placing a high value on integrity, transparency, and the user-centric commitment to improving academic success. 

As we develop StudentShare, there are a few questions that we must ask ourselves throughout the design process (and after) to ensure that our decisions support our core values. First we must ask: Is this feature useful for students; does this feature contribute to their academic success? If we know a feature is useful and adds value to the student’s academic career, we must then consider accessibility and responsible use. Is this feature inclusive to all users; who does our technology harm or exclude? Lastly, we must assess responsible use by considering how our application could be misused. Asking ourselves these questions throughout the development lifecycle will help us ensure that our actions align with our values.
