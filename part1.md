StudentShare is a P2P software company specializing in academic file sharing. Because our technology uses a P2P network, we will rely on users of StudentShare to ultimately build our platform through use and interaction. Over the course of the next five years, we hope to establish a user base at all CSU and UC campuses in the state of California. If we can establish a user base at these major universities in California, we will be one step closer to achieving our long-term goal: creating a student hub that supports academic success through file and resource sharing.

Our idea of creating a student hub originated from our own student experiences. File sharing is often needed within a group setting, and many services available to us require a login that slows down the process of document transfers. We wanted to create a technology that would allow instant access to document-sharing features, skipping the login process and allowing for a more efficient collaborative experience. The need for quicker, less personal file sharing was the primary motivator for our development of StudentShare. However, as we delved deeper into the student experience and what we thought was missing from it, we identified a few other areas of interest that led us to conceive of StudentShare as not only a file-sharing service, but also a student hub that could be used to meet other needs. In our experience, note-taking is a time-consuming process. As students, we could benefit from shared study materials that reduce the need to take extensive notes. Lastly, in our experience, buying textbooks and other supplies is costly, and these resources are sometimes only needed for a short period of time. While there are many online resources available to buy and sell used textbooks and other materials, many of these services connect students with sellers in distant locations, increasing shipping and handling costs.
