# Project Overview

## Project Description

This project examines how ethics can be incorporated into the creation and operation of a technology startup. 
Your group will create a fictitious but realistic technology company that develops a feasible technology product or service.  
Treat your company as if it were a real startup. Your group will develop an Ethical Business Plan that integrates the individual  
work completed by group members in Project 3. You will also develop a Cultural Policy and an Ethics Policy. The goal is to demonstrate  
how ethical considerations can influence a technology company's strategy, decisions, safeguards, culture, and organizational policies.
